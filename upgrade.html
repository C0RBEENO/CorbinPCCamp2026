The What

I would like to upgrade the storage on my computer

The Why

I personally like to play a variety of games, keep a variety of recordings, and overall keep things downloaded. By upgrading the amount of storage I have I expand the amount of things I can have downloaded which is a huge quality of life upgrade for me.

The Upgrade

The SSD I would personally upgrade to is the Kingston NV3 1 TB M.2-2280 PCIe 4.0 X4 NVME Solid State Drive. This is because it has a terabyte of storage which for the extent of what the pc can run and what I desire to run on it is more than enough. It is also compatible with the motherboard and has high reviews.
